06 Straight answers

Questions we get asked

How can a small studio deliver this fast?

Years of building our own products means the repetitive parts of a web build are already solved — our testing, deployment and QA tooling is written and battle-tested. We spend our time on the parts that are specific to your business, not reinventing the plumbing. Every client-facing deliverable is reviewed and approved by a person before it ships, and you always have someone to call.

Do I own the website?

Completely. You own the domain, the hosting account and the code. Every credential and the documentation are handed over at launch. We don't hold sites hostage behind a monthly fee — care plans are optional and cancellable any time.

What if I don't like it?

The first payment covers the build to a reviewable state. If what you see doesn't match what we agreed in writing, you don't pay the second half and owe nothing further. Two rounds of revisions are included in every package.

Why is this cheaper than an agency?

No office, no sales team, no account managers, and automation on the repetitive parts. You're paying for the build, not the overhead wrapped around it.

How long does it take?

Starter Site: typically 1–2 weeks from approved scope. Growth Site: 2–4 weeks. Automation work depends on how many systems are involved and gets a firm date in the written quote. If a date is going to slip, you hear it from us before it does.

Do I have to take a care plan?

No. Every project includes 30 days free, then it continues at $149/mo unless you cancel — and cancelling is one email. Plenty of clients self-manage. We just won't pretend an unmaintained site keeps performing.
